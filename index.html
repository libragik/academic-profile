AGY Research Project • 6–12 Month Roadmap

Agentic Acceleration of Foundation Models on Heterogeneous Scientific Datasets

Computational / Dry Lab Gemini 4 Argon • Google Antigravity Non-Commercial Open Science
1

Core Problem & Bottleneck

Benchmarking frontier foundation models on non-Euclidean spatial-temporal scientific datasets suffers from brittle pipeline orchestration, fragmented GPU experiment tracking, and massive contextual overhead during iterative model debugging across distributed dry-lab environments.

2

Approach via Google Antigravity & Argon

Using Google Antigravity’s agentic orchestration alongside Gemini 4 Argon, we deploy autonomous code-refactoring agents, closed-loop distributed training diagnostic monitors, and automated hyperparameter validation across heterogeneous graph-structured datasets.

3

Milestones & Concrete Deliverables

Over the next 6–12 months, this program produces:

  • Preprints & Peer-Reviewed Publications: Target submissions to premier AI & scientific discovery venues (NeurIPS / ICLR track).
  • Open-Source Benchmarking Suite: Public GitHub library providing reproducible baseline scripts and autonomous diagnostic harnesses.
  • Curated Scientific Dataset & Model Checkpoints: Structurally validated spatial-temporal graph representations deposited on Zenodo with DOI.
